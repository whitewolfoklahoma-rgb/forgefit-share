Please read first: this offline version does not connect live to your watch or phone app, and it does not sync in the background. Instead, you save a file from your device app, then pick that file here — LogEasy reads it right on this device and nothing is uploaded. A live one-tap sync belongs to a future connected edition; it is not in this version.

Step 1 of 3 — Pick your device or app

Pick one, then tap Next. Going back never loses the device you picked.

Auto Import Device Data — pick your file

Tap the box below and choose the export file you just saved. You can choose more than one file at once. LogEasy detects Apple Health XML, Fitbit CSV or JSON, other activity JSON, and generic CSV, then fills in every supported field it finds. This is import from a file you saved — not live sync.

Advanced: individual importers, fallback & connected sources

Use these if Auto Import cannot identify a file. An unusual CSV opens the column-mapping fallback. ForgeKitchen and High-Protein Pack meal JSON also work through Auto Import or the meal importer below.

Apple Health export (.xml)

Reads steps, active energy, total calories when resting + active energy are present, distance, exercise time, workouts, sleep, weight and resting heart rate, grouped by day.

Fitbit daily CSV

Reads the Fitbit daily summary: date, steps, total calories burned, activity calories, distance, active minutes and sleep.

Generic activity CSV

Any CSV with date plus steps, active calories, total calories, distance, exercise minutes, workouts, sleep, weight or resting heart rate columns (Garmin, Samsung, Google Fit exports, spreadsheets).

Import from ForgeKitchen — meal (.json)

Pairs with ForgeKitchen, our companion recipe app: export a meal there, import it here, and it appears in that day's food log with calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber and sodium counted. (The offline editions connect by sending/importing a file — there's no live sync.)

Help & support

Short, plain how-tos for the jobs LogEasy is used for every day. Tap a topic to open it.

Log food by voice

On the Today page (or in the Nutrition tab), tap “Tell me what you ate” and say what you had, like “two eggs and toast”. LogEasy shows you the matches it found — check them, change a quantity if you need to, then tap Add for each one. Nothing is saved until you tap Add. If your browser can't use the microphone, typing works just as well (see the next topic).

Log food by typing

Go to the Nutrition tab, type the food in the search box, pick the meal, and tap Add. Eating something that isn't listed? Open “Add a custom food” on that same page, fill in the name and the numbers you know, and tap Save custom food — then you can search for it and add it like any other food.

Your day at a glance & the next-step card

The four big cards at the top of the Today page show calories left today, steps today, last night's sleep and your current weight — all from your own logs and imports. A card showing “—” means LogEasy doesn't have that number yet; the line under it says how to add it. Tap any card to jump to that number's page. Under the cards, one next-step card suggests the single most useful thing left today (like logging lunch). Tap the ✕ to dismiss it — it stays away for the rest of the day.

Re-log fast: favorites, recents & copy yesterday

In the Nutrition tab, the Favorites & recent list sits above the search results. Tap the ☆ beside any food (in search results or the quick list) to pin it as a favorite; your most recently logged foods appear automatically. Set a quantity — the 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 buttons fill it in for you — and tap Add to log it again in one go. Ate the same as yesterday? Open the food log and tap Copy yesterday → this day to copy the whole day across; you can still delete any item you didn't have.

Adaptive target suggestions

Once you have about two weeks of weigh-ins, LogEasy compares your actual weight trend with your goal pace and may show a target suggestion card on the Today page — with the math and the weigh-ins it used printed right on the card. Nothing ever changes on its own: your calorie target only moves if you tap Use … kcal. Tap Not now and it won't ask again for a week. The suggestion is figured on your device from your own weigh-ins; it is an estimate, not medical advice. If you turn a suggestion on, the card tells you it's active and offers a Remove adjustment button.

Log a workout

Go to the Train tab. Start today's planned workout, pick a day from your month plan, or tap Log workout to start an empty session and add exercises as you go. Fill in the weight and reps for each set, tick the sets you did, then tap Finish & save workout. You can also tap “Tell me what you trained” and say it — you still confirm it before anything is saved.

Import device data

Go to the Devices tab and follow the three steps in Connect your device data: pick your device or app, save an export file from that app using the steps shown, then pick that file in LogEasy and read the confirmation of what came in. This version imports a file — it does not sync live with your watch or phone app.

Back up / restore your data

Go to the Progress tab, open Backup & reports, and tap Export backup (JSON) to save a backup file. Keep that file somewhere safe. To restore it later — on this device or a new one — open the same place, choose the backup file under Restore a backup, and your logs come back.

All of your LogEasy data lives only on this device, in this browser/app — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Back it up with Progress → Backup & reports → Export backup (JSON) before you switch phones or browsers, and keep the backup file somewhere safe.

ℹ️ About LogEasy

LogEasy version 2.5.0 is an on-device coaching engine: your month plan, targets and feedback are computed from your profile and logs by built-in training and nutrition rules (Mifflin-St Jeor energy math, progressive overload, macro-based meal suggestions). Month plans use conservative healthy-rate limits — fat loss is capped at no more than 1% of body weight per week and gain at no more than 0.5% per week — and targets never go below the app's 1,200-calorie floor. Weight projections use the rough 3,500 kcal ≈ 1 lb approximation; they are estimates, not guarantees or medical claims. No account, no cloud, no subscription — everything is stored only in this browser on this device. Export a backup from the Progress tab before switching browsers or devices. Coaching is general fitness guidance for healthy adults, not medical advice — check with a doctor before starting a new program, especially with any health condition.
